## Honest Assessment First

Ranking for "transformation" or "delivery" as standalone terms is not realistic. Those searches return KPMG, Deloitte, McKinsey, and FTSE 100 company pages. No amount of on-page SEO will shift that.

That is not the goal, and it does not need to be. The recruiters and hiring managers looking for someone like Carl do not search "transformation" and scroll 40 pages. They search something much more specific. That is where the opportunity sits.

## What Needs to Change on the Site

### 1. Title tag — current vs proposed
**Current:** `Carl Robinson | Chief Delivery Officer | Chief Transformation Officer`
**Proposed:** `Carl Robinson — Interim CDO & CTrO | Chief Delivery Officer | Transformation Leadership UK`

Rationale: "Interim" is a key qualifier. "UK" signals geography to search engines. Adds long-tail terms without stuffing.

### 2. Meta description — current vs proposed
**Current:** `Carl Robinson. CDO and CTrO. Built Globant UK from $13m to $290m+ in 4 years. Available now for interim or permanent senior leadership.`
**Proposed:** `Interim CDO and Chief Transformation Officer available now. Built Globant UK from $13m to $290m+. Delivery, transformation, and portfolio leadership at board level across financial services, healthcare, and technology.`

Rationale: Opens with the searchable terms. Keeps the credibility anchor (Globant). Adds sector terms for relevance signals.

### 3. Schema.org — enhancement
Currently has basic Person schema. Needs to be extended with:
- `description` field
- `knowsAbout` array: "Digital Transformation", "Delivery Leadership", "Portfolio Management", "Programme Management", "Organisational Change"
- `hasOccupation` with `occupationLocation` set to UK
- `alumniOf` / `worksFor` history to build entity signals

### 4. Page copy — keyword weaving
The current copy is strong and human. It does not need to be rewritten. But a few specific additions would help:

- Add "UK" once in the hero section where it fits naturally (Google uses geography signals)
- Add "interim or permanent" explicitly in the contact/CTA section (this is what people search)
- The numbers section should explicitly use "transformation" and "delivery" in headings, not just in the body text

### 5. Alt text
Any images on the site (headshot, etc.) need descriptive alt text: `Carl Robinson, Chief Delivery Officer and Chief Transformation Officer, UK`

### 6. Internal linking and structure
The site is currently a single page. That's fine for UX. But it means Google has one URL to index. The medium-term fix is a simple `/articles` or `/thinking` section — even 3-4 short pieces would create additional indexed URLs with keyword-rich content.

## Content as an SEO Lever (Medium Term)

A blog/articles section is the single biggest lever available. Not because it drives traffic directly, but because it:
- Creates additional indexed pages
- Builds topical authority in transformation/delivery/CDO space
- Captures long-tail searches around specific topics
- Gives LinkedIn posts a natural home (link from LinkedIn to article = backlink)

This does not have to be complex. A `/thinking` page with 4-5 short pieces at launch would be enough to start. Topics could mirror what Carl already posts on LinkedIn — adapted for a permanent, searchable format.

---

## Backlink Strategy

Google ranks pages partly by who links to them. For a personal exec site, the achievable sources are:

**Immediate (low effort):**
- Ensure LinkedIn profile links to carlrobinson.me — and that the URL in the schema.org `sameAs` matches exactly
- Add carlrobinson.me to any professional body profiles (APM, IoD, etc.) if Carl has listings there
- Add to any speaking/event bios where Carl has been featured

**Medium term:**
- Guest articles in professional publications (APM, Changeboard, etc.) — even one piece with a bio link is a quality backlink
- LinkedIn articles that reference carlrobinson.me in the body
- Any press mentions, case studies, or podcast appearances — always request a link to the site

## Google Search Console Review

The site is already verified and a sitemap is in place. At the start of the SEO session:
1. Open Search Console (search.google.com/search-console)
2. Review: Queries report — what terms are already bringing impressions or clicks?
3. Coverage — are all pages indexed?
4. Core Web Vitals — any performance issues flagged?
5. Request indexing for any pages that were recently updated

This takes 20 minutes and gives the baseline before making changes.
